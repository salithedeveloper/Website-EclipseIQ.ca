EclipseIQ Technologies Inc. draws on decades of executive leadership and hands-on experience across insurance technology, operations and digital transformation.

We help insurers, MGAs, reinsurers, brokers and specialty insurance organizations modernize systems and improve how work gets done. Based in Canada, we work with organizations here and internationally.

Our work spans strategic consulting, solution architecture, implementation and fractional CIO / COO / CTO leadership. We also develop EclipseSME™, our policy administration platform.
