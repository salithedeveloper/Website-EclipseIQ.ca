Ask us directly
Who do you work with?+

We work with insurers, MGAs, reinsurers, brokers, program administrators and specialty insurance organizations, from emerging businesses to established enterprises.

Do you work outside Canada?+

Yes. EclipseIQ is based in Canada and supports insurance organizations in Canada and internationally.

Can you help with our existing systems?+

Yes. We assess and modernize legacy insurance systems through planning, enhancements, integrations and data migration. The approach depends on your business priorities and existing technology landscape.

Do you implement platforms other than EclipseSME?+

Yes. We provide implementation, integration and advisory services for third-party insurance platforms, as well as our own EclipseSME platform.

What is the Eclipse Totality Assessment™?+

It is a review of your insurance technology landscape, processes, data, integrations, governance and workflows. The assessment identifies risks and opportunities and produces a prioritized roadmap for improvement.

Can you support data conversion and migration?+

Yes. We help assess, cleanse, map, transform, validate and migrate data for system implementations, upgrades and modernization projects.

What does fractional technology leadership involve?+

Fractional CIO and technology leadership can include technology strategy, transformation oversight, vendor selection and aligning technology investments with business objectives. The scope is tailored to your needs.

What is EclipseSME™?+

EclipseSME is an insurance platform developed by EclipseIQ. It supports underwriting, quoting, policy administration, renewals, endorsements, document generation and operational workflows for specialty insurers, MGAs and program administrators.

Can we arrange an EclipseSME demonstration?+

Yes. Use the contact form and choose EclipseSME demonstration so we can discuss the workflows and requirements that matter to your organization.

How are pricing and timelines determined?+

Scope, system complexity, migration needs and integration requirements shape the proposal and schedule. These are discussed before work begins; there is no one-size-fits-all implementation timeline.

Do you provide custom solutions?+

Yes. Alongside configurable EclipseSME workflows, products, business rules, forms and integrations, we provide custom development and consulting for specialized requirements.
