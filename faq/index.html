What products and services do you offer?

We provide insurance technology consulting, systems modernization, solution architecture, digital transformation, fractional CIO / COO / CTO leadership, assessments, coaching, audit readiness and security reviews. EclipseSME™ is our insurance platform for specialty organizations.

Which insurance organizations do you work with?

We work with insurers, MGAs, reinsurers, brokers, program administrators and other specialty insurance organizations, from emerging businesses to established enterprises.

Can I arrange an EclipseSME demonstration?

Yes. Use the contact form to request an EclipseSME demonstration. We can discuss your requirements and focus the conversation on the features and workflows relevant to your organization.

What is your pricing model?

Pricing is tailored to your requirements. Consulting and implementation proposals set out the scope, deliverables, timeline and pricing before work begins.

Do you work outside Canada?

Yes. EclipseIQ is based in Canada and works with insurance organizations in Canada and internationally.

What is EclipseSME™, and how can it help?

EclipseSME is a cloud-based insurance platform developed by EclipseIQ for specialty insurers, MGAs and program administrators. It supports underwriting, quoting, policy administration, renewals, endorsements, document generation and operational workflows.

How long does an EclipseSME implementation take?

Timelines depend on product complexity, data migration and integration requirements. The current website describes many implementations as taking 6–12 weeks, with more complex projects requiring longer. An assessment establishes a plan for your project; this is an indicative range, not a delivery guarantee.

Do you offer customized solutions?

Yes. EclipseSME supports configurable products, workflows, forms, business rules, reporting and integrations. We also provide custom development and consulting for specialized requirements.

Do you implement other insurance technology platforms?

Yes. Alongside EclipseSME, we provide implementation, integration and advisory services for third-party insurance platforms. The business requirement shapes the solution.

Can you assist with data conversion and migration?

Yes. We help assess, cleanse, map, transform, validate and migrate data during implementations, upgrades and modernization. This includes EclipseSME, other platforms and system consolidation.

How do you approach digital transformation?

We start with your objectives, operational challenges and technology landscape. Together, we develop a practical roadmap that may involve process improvement, modernization, automation, data and analytics, cloud technology and organizational change.

What is the Eclipse Totality Assessment™?

It is a comprehensive review of your technology landscape, business processes, data, integrations, governance and operational workflows. It identifies risks and improvement opportunities and concludes with a prioritized roadmap. The original homepage also calls it the Totality Check.

Do you provide fractional technology leadership?

Yes. Fractional CIO / COO / CTO leadership can support technology strategy, operational alignment, transformation oversight, vendor selection and investment decisions. Scope is tailored to the organization and its priorities.

Can you modernize legacy insurance systems?

Yes. We assess and improve legacy systems through strategic planning, enhancements, integrations, data migration and transformation initiatives, balancing business priorities with practical implementation.
