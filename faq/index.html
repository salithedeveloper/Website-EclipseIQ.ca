Software & implementation

Products, customization and delivery.

What insurance software do you offer?

EclipseSME™ is our flagship product: configurable cloud software for underwriting, policy administration, billing and reporting. It is designed for MGAs, insurers and specialty insurance organizations.

Do you work with platforms other than EclipseSME?

Yes. We provide advisory, implementation, integration and modernization services for third-party insurance platforms, as well as custom development.

Do you provide custom software solutions?

Yes. We develop software and integrations for specialized insurance requirements. We also configure EclipseSME products, workflows, forms and business rules to fit your operations.

Can you modernize legacy insurance systems?

Yes. We assess legacy systems and support enhancements, integrations, custom development and data migration. We plan the work around your business priorities and existing operations.

How long does implementation take, and how is a project scoped?

Timelines depend on product complexity, configuration, integrations and data migration. After assessing your requirements, we agree the scope, deliverables, implementation plan and timeline before work begins.

Working with EclipseIQ

Expertise, engagements and practical details.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. EclipseIQ is based in Canada and works with insurance organizations in Canada and internationally. Engagements are scoped around each organization’s business and technology requirements.

What does fractional leadership involve?

Fractional CIO / COO / CTO engagements provide senior technology or operational leadership without a full-time executive appointment. The scope may include strategy, governance, investment decisions, delivery oversight and team development.

What is the Eclipse Totality Assessment™?

A review of your systems, processes, data, integrations and governance. It identifies risks and opportunities and provides a prioritized improvement roadmap.

How are services and software priced?

Pricing is based on your requirements. Consulting and implementation proposals set out scope, deliverables, timeline and pricing before work begins. Contact us to discuss software configuration and implementation needs.
