# Enterprise design research and application

Reviewed 24 October 2026. This is implementation evidence, not a request for the owner to review reference websites.

## Critical assessment of the previous presentation

The positioning and factual content were useful. The presentation remained weak for five specific reasons:

1. The opening was dominated by a small, box-based capability diagram. It read as a placeholder rather than a corporate brand composition.
2. Miniature interface-like illustrations were repeated in the hero and product feature, reducing variety and implying product detail the illustrations could not demonstrate.
3. Small labels, thin borders and a single rounded geometric typeface gave most sections the same visual weight.
4. The service offering changed between several box styles without establishing a clear visual relationship between proprietary products and independent professional services.
5. Interior pages lacked the visual hierarchy and navigation support of a substantial corporate site.

## Applied creative direction

- Full-width engineering imagery gives the introduction visual substance while the heading remains moderately sized.
- Equal Products and Services entry points immediately explain the two business areas.
- Our Offerings pairs a substantial software panel with three open service practices in the required order. Third-party implementation is explicit.
- A commissioned, tactile software concept replaces miniature interface diagrams. The caption and alt text identify it as an illustration, not an EclipseSME screenshot.
- Manrope headings are paired with self-hosted IBM Plex Sans body text for a clearer corporate and technical character.
- Navy experience/footer chapters, pale blue product surfaces and open white practice sections create deliberate variation.
- Services has a desktop practice index; FAQ retains grouped native disclosures; Contact retains the same processing and privacy controls.

No wholesale factual rewrite, speculative product publication, employee claims or deployment is included.
