# Current direction: a corporate identity for products and professional services

This document supersedes previous visual-direction notes. The implementation remains in the actual root `index.html`, existing pages, shared stylesheet and source templates. No new concepts or review directory.

## What changed

The previous small capability diagrams and repetitive nested boxes have been replaced by a photographic engineering introduction, equally prominent product/service discovery routes, a purpose-built offerings composition and an original software concept render. The original corporate logo and established blue/turquoise family remain unchanged.

The navigation is **Products, Services, About, FAQ, Contact**. The homepage introduces the company, exposes both business areas, presents the four offerings, features EclipseSME, explains insurance experience and provides concise FAQ/contact routes.

## Products and services

EclipseIQ remains an insurance technology products and services company. EclipseSME is the only published product. The catalogue is data-driven and requires explicit `published: true`; drafts stay hidden. The product has a substantial illustrated feature, audience/purpose copy, and direct links to its website and demo page.

Implementation, integration and modernization explicitly cover EclipseIQ and third-party insurance platforms. Consulting retains InsurTech strategy, architecture, transformation, Totality Assessment, audit readiness and security reviews. Fractional leadership retains CIO/COO/CTO governance, operational leadership and team development. No unsupported clients, partnerships, credentials, case studies or metrics are added.

## Professional visual system

Manrope headings, IBM Plex Sans body type, controlled navy/turquoise contrast, consistent action styling and readable body text are shared across the site. The opening hero uses commissioned engineering imagery, not handshake stock photography or circuitry. The product image is a clearly identified concept illustration, not a real application screenshot. The Services page uses a desktop practice index, About combines engineering context with experience, FAQ retains native expandable groups, and Contact remains accessible and familiar.

See [enterprise-design-research.md](enterprise-design-research.md) for the critique of the old presentation, the nine requested industry references and the principles applied. The research informed presentation standards, not claims about EclipseIQ's scale.

## Foundations retained

Form controls, validation, spam safeguards, contact preferences, local simulation, fail-closed production configuration, consent and analytics preparation remain. Topic values are stable. No public email address is displayed. Canonical/social metadata, Organization schema, sitemap, local-file paths and legacy policy aliases remain.

Nothing is deployed. The live WordPress website and EclipseSME repository are untouched.
