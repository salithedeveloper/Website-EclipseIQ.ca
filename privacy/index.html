Website privacy notice / 23 October 2026

Information we collect and use

EclipseIQ Technologies Inc. uses the details you provide—such as your name, email address, company, phone number and message—to respond to inquiries, discuss demonstrations and service requests, and communicate about your requirements. Only share a high-level overview; do not include confidential or policyholder information.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers helping operate this website and respond to inquiries, or shared where required by law. Appropriate handling and security matter, but no internet transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Contact form providers

When live delivery is configured, inquiries are processed through Formspree. Optional Google reCAPTCHA may protect the form from spam if enabled. These services process information needed for delivery and verification. Review Formspree’s privacy policy (new tab) and, when enabled, Google’s privacy policy (new tab).

Local previews identify simulated submissions clearly; they do not deliver inquiries. Hosted delivery remains unavailable until configured with EclipseIQ’s own endpoint.

Cookies and browser storage

This site stores your optional analytics preference in your browser for up to 180 days. This essential preference helps respect your choice. If storage is unavailable, your selection applies to the current page only.

Optional analytics remains off until you accept. The site is prepared for EclipseIQ’s Google Analytics 4 property, but no analytics runs without its configured Measurement ID. With consent, page-view data uses the canonical page address and page title. The implementation does not deliberately include form contents, query strings or URL fragments. Advertising features are disabled.

Change or withdraw consent

Use Cookie preferences in the footer to accept or reject optional analytics at any time. Rejecting after acceptance disables collection and reloads the page to unload the analytics code. Accessible first-party analytics cookies are removed where possible. The site cannot delete third-party or HttpOnly cookies or remove information already collected.

Technical information and external sites

The hosting provider may process IP addresses, browser information and access logs when serving this website. External sites have their own privacy practices. Links opening in a new tab are identified.

Your privacy questions and requests

You may ask about information you have shared, request access, correction or deletion, or ask to stop optional marketing communications. Use the contact form for an initial request without including sensitive details. EclipseIQ can follow up to verify and address it as appropriate.

Updates to this notice

This notice may be updated as website features and practices change. Please review it periodically. It replaces WordPress-specific cookie descriptions for this static website.
