A concise explanation of how this website handles inquiries and optional analytics.

Website notice / 23 October 2026

Contact inquiries

When you send an inquiry, EclipseIQ Technologies Inc. uses the information you provide to respond and discuss your requirements. Only provide information you are comfortable sharing for this purpose. Do not submit sensitive personal, policyholder or confidential business information.

Once live delivery is configured, inquiries are processed through Formspree. Optional spam verification may use Google reCAPTCHA if enabled. These providers process information needed to deliver and protect the form. Review Formspree’s privacy policy (new tab) and, if verification is enabled, Google’s privacy policy (new tab).

In local development, the form clearly identifies simulated submissions. They are not delivered. On a hosted site, the form remains unavailable until a valid delivery configuration is supplied.

Essential storage

This website remembers your analytics preference in browser storage for up to 180 days. This preference is necessary to respect your choice. If browser storage is unavailable, your choice applies to the current page only.

Optional analytics

Optional analytics is off until you accept. The site is prepared for Google Analytics 4, but collection remains inactive until EclipseIQ’s own Measurement ID is configured. When enabled with your consent, analytics uses the canonical page address and page title; form contents, query strings and fragments are not deliberately included in page-view data. Advertising features are disabled.

Change or withdraw consent

Choose Cookie preferences in the footer to accept or reject optional analytics at any time. Rejecting after acceptance disables collection and reloads the page to unload the analytics script. Accessible first-party analytics cookies are removed where the browser permits. The site cannot delete third-party or HttpOnly cookies, and withdrawal does not remove data already collected.

Hosting and external links

Your hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP addresses and access logs when serving this site. External websites have their own privacy practices. Links open in a new tab where indicated.

Questions about your information

Use the contact form for questions or requests concerning information you have shared with EclipseIQ. Please avoid including sensitive details in your initial request.
