# EclipseIQ homepage concept research

Research performed 23 October 2026 (America/Toronto). This review starts from a new creative brief, not the existing redesign's layout or CSS. All additions are isolated in `review/homepage-concepts/`.

## Company and content

- [Live corporate homepage](https://eclipseiq.ca/): insurance technology consulting, fractional leadership, digital transformation, platform ownership, Canadian base, executive and hands-on insurance experience. Reviewed the live HTML and published content. The original website is a content and asset reference only.
- [Corporate FAQ](https://eclipseiq.ca/faq/): repository-verified FAQ content supports custom development, third-party systems integration, data migration, systems modernization and Eclipse Totality Assessment™.
- [EclipseSME](https://eclipsesme.com/): reviewed current live HTML and stylesheet, plus a local browser rendering of those downloaded sources. The rendering used local Manrope in place of any unavailable remote font and disabled remote scripts; it was a visual reference, not a live functional audit. Noted the product site's substantial capability illustration, layered dark surfaces, explicit insurance language and alternating content sections. The corporate concepts have their own compositions and blue/turquoise palette. No EclipseSME repository access or changes.
- Existing repository: `scripts/content.json`, `docs/assets.md`, `docs/live-audit-and-gap-analysis.md`, `docs/discovery.md`, and published home/services/about/FAQ content. The historical discovery document contains superseded branding notes, so current asset documentation and live sources take precedence. Existing implementation files remain unchanged.

Copy is rewritten from verified material. No customer names, testimonials, certification claims, performance figures, implementation guarantees or invented leadership biographies are added. Diagrams are conceptual capability illustrations, not product interfaces. The planning photo is an existing corporate asset, not a claim that the people pictured are EclipseIQ staff.

## Brand and asset provenance

- `assets/logo.png`: byte-identical copy of the repository's actual corporate logo, originally from [the live WordPress asset](https://eclipseiq.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/EclipseIQ-Logo-202-x-88-px.png). No tracing, cropping, recolouring or inversion. Kept on white at or below original size.
- Established palette: `#023047` deep blue, `#219ebc` turquoise and `#13a8ff` logo blue, supported by accessible darker text blues and pale blue surfaces. The logo's original colours remain intact.
- `assets/insurance-team.png`: downloaded unchanged from [the corporate planning photograph](https://eclipseiq.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/EIS_BrainStorming.png). Used only in Concept 02; composed with CSS object-fit.
- `assets/manrope.woff2`: existing repository's self-hosted Manrope; SIL Open Font License copied alongside it. Concept 02 adds system Georgia as an editorial accent. No runtime font service.
- `assets/architecture.svg` and `assets/operations.svg`: original concept-specific illustrations. Product diagrams are illustrative and labelled accordingly, without fabricated data.

## Three creative directions

1. **Integrated Intelligence:** dark architecture-led hero; cool technical palette; a layered model relating leadership, engineering and software; asymmetrical capabilities presentation; a substantial light product section; clear executive experience narrative. Recommended for the strongest corporate technology positioning.
2. **Executive Perspective:** light editorial hero; existing planning photograph; restrained serif accents; typographic service columns; a contrasting executive expertise section; an illustrated product folio. Best if advisory and fractional executive engagements should lead the brand.
3. **Connected Operations:** light blue panoramic hero; an insurance operating model; outcome-based capability rows; a saturated blue product section; explicit supporting assurance services. Best for quickly explaining the company's role across insurance operations. Mobile uses a purpose-designed vertical model.

## Scope and interaction

Each is a substantial single-page design with navigation, hero, services, platform, company context and contact CTA. Internal navigation stays within the concept. Contact and demo CTAs link to the existing corporate/product sites; they do not create submissions from this review. Comparison selectors change local screenshot panels. No analytics, forms, tracking, deployment, live website modification, new internal pages or EclipseSME repository changes.
